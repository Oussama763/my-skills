# Universal Brainstorming

Guide users through structured, multi-dimensional ideation across technical, creative, product, and strategic domains.

## Ideation Frameworks

Select or combine frameworks based on the context:

*   **Divergent Phase (Quantity & Variety):**
    *   **SCAMPER:** Substitute, Combine, Adapt, Modify, Put to another use, Eliminate, Reverse.
    *   **First Principles:** Strip down to core constraints and rebuild without legacy assumptions.
    *   **Inversion:** Map out how to fail completely, then invert those strategies to discover non-obvious solutions.

*   **Convergent Phase (Refinement & Evaluation):**
    *   **Impact vs. Effort Matrix:** Group ideas into Quick Wins, Major Projects, Fill-ins, and Hard Slogs.
    *   **Feasibility vs. Innovation:** Evaluate risk, technical complexity, and novel potential.

## Domain Approaches

*   **Technical & Engineering:** Focus on trade-offs, scalability, edge cases, system bottlenecks, and simple baseline prototypes.
*   **Product & Features:** Focus on target user personas, core pain points, feature trade-offs, and clear success metrics.
*   **Creative & Narrative:** Explore storytelling angles, thematic hooks, aesthetic contrast, and emotional resonance.

## Workflow

1.  **Frame the Problem:** Define the underlying goal, key constraints, and desired outcome in 1-2 sentences.
2.  **Divergent Generation:** Produce 5-10 distinct concepts spanning safe, wild, and orthogonal angles.
3.  **Synthesize & Categorize:** Structure ideas into actionable categories or an Impact vs. Effort breakdown.
4.  **Next Steps:** Pick 1-2 top candidates and propose concrete action items or prototype experiments.
